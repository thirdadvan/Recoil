Warehouse rack ladders and Freight local sound (Unity)

All four Steel rack - pallets and freight objects have Left rack ladder - editable as a child. Each includes grouped rails/rungs, a trigger area and the existing ClimbableLadder component. Height 5, width .6, left offset 1.8; editing/moving the rack carries its ladder. Reusable prefab: Prefabs/WarehouseRackLadder.prefab.

Escort restriction is enforced both at RecoilPrototype.FindLadder and RecoilPlayerController input/active-climb handling. Taking a child's hand releases an active ladder. Normal solo ladder climbing remains.

FreightNoiseEmitter is attached to the KBKStackerCrane object and reusable crane prefab. When powered and moving (raise/travel/lower), it emits immediately, then every player footstepInterval. Radius, propagation speed, line width and colour match PlayerNoiseEmitter. Default footstep values: radius 3.2, interval .45s, speed 10. The sound origin follows travelRig at its ground-level base; Inspector soundOrigin can override it. Idle and loss of power stop new pulses. Existing pulses finish normally; game pause stops their simulation.

LocalSoundWaveEmitter is the shared ripple rendering/hearing helper used by PlayerNoiseEmitter and FreightNoiseEmitter. Player/child sound timings remain owned by their actor scripts. Freight noise wakes and directs nearby monsters through existing HearNoise, with the same local propagation as footsteps.

Warehouse lighting has now been converted to RecoilIndustrialLamp (downward soft cone and radial spill); see IndustrialLighting-Notes.txt. Fixture transforms and authored radius/intensity were preserved.

Checked in Play: four ladder components, solo climb, escort ladder refusal; 3 Freight pulses over 1.2s, sleeping monster hears Freight, no new pulses when idle/unpowered, player hearing still works after shared helper extraction. No script compilation errors. Backup: Backups/WarehouseRackLadders.
