Indoor RecoilFactoryInterior: Four-storey interior additions
Ground floor layout and five existing Rampagers preserved. Upper floors now have public corridors, 2 independent room scenes per floor,
and one open circulation alcove. See FactoryNestedRooms-Notes.txt. Storey foot heights: 0, 7.6, 11.6, 15.6. Ground floor remains 7.6m; upper floors are 4m.
Right Stairwell - continuous switchbacks has 3 WalkableStairway components.

WalkableStairway routePoints are ordered FOOT positions, lower to upper, including
halfway return landing. W moves towards the upper endpoint, S towards the lower.
A/D passes entrances without automatically taking stairs. Entry approaches smoothly.
Release W/S to pause. Space or a successful shot leaves stairs. Monster hits detach.
Speed, entry radius and entry height tolerance are editable per component.
Shift/crouch slow movement and suppress footsteps. Supported stair travel refills ammo.
Stair path owns position during traversal; no Rigidbody2D or Physics2D solver change.
