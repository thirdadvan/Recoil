Warehouse distribution box / power routing puzzle

Source: user IMG_3479.HEIC drawing, and distribution-board structure references:
https://www.education.gov.za/LinkClick.aspx?fileticket=tWZlPjCKl2U%3D&mid=12062&portalid=0&tabid=4347
https://www.se.com/ca/en/download/document/80043-741-03/
The UI represents incoming supply, branch breakers, outgoing cables, neutral and earth bars. Routing Freight through Lighting is a simplified game puzzle.

Outside: Warehouse distribution box - interactive, child of Factory Warehouse - exterior, near the entrance. E opens the new ElectricalPanelInteraction page; E/Esc or Close closes it. The scene context pauses gameplay while this modal page is active, including enemies and player firing.

Initial: Lighting cable connected and powered. Freight source burned and not supplying power. AC/Gate/Ventilation damaged.
No ON/OFF switch, trip animation, or separate energize step. Cable insertion supplies power immediately; unplugging cuts it.
Both coloured cables: first click unplugs at the terminal underside, next click picks up into a fixed suspended pose. Click a compatible free terminal to reinsert; any other click, E/Esc or Close puts it back at its disconnected position.
Lighting cable can be restored to the free Lighting terminal. Freight can be restored to its original burned terminal, but gets no power there.
Freight inserted into free Lighting terminal: bypass supplies Freight, leaving warehouse lighting disconnected. Only the Lighting supply indicator on the panel glows green; the burned Freight indicator stays dark. The machine operation light shows actual Freight power separately.
Lighting terminal accepts one cable at a time. To restore warehouse lighting after bypass, unplug Freight and reinsert Lighting.
Disconnected Lighting cable is offset beside the bypass so both remain visible and clickable.
Power loss pauses the crane; reconnecting Freight to Lighting resumes it.

Scripts:
ElectricalPanelInteraction -> outside distribution box: page rendering and click routing.
WarehousePowerState -> independent puzzle model and progress flags.
RecoilPrototype -> modal ownership, paused simulation, input routing and travel/retry persistence.
WarehouseLightingPower -> warehouse world root: disables the normal warehouse lights when Lighting is disconnected.
KBKStackerCrane -> existing crane root: requiredPowerItem=warehouse_power_freight_on; unavailable before rewiring. Powered idle operation indicator is green.
KBKCraneSwitch -> fixed ground control: reports missing power until the circuit works.

All progress uses warehouse_power_ flags carried by existing scene travel. Retry preserves these flags; a fresh game starts with the original wiring.
Reusable prefab: Assets/RecoilPrototype/Prefabs/WarehouseDistributionBox.prefab
This feature is implemented in Unity. The JavaScript prototype has not been extended with this puzzle yet.
