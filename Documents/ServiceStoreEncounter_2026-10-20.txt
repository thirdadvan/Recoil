Service store: clerk / hidden observer tutorial

Scene: Assets/Scenes/RecoilServiceStore.unity
Editable hierarchy: RECOIL - Service Store / Store Clerk Encounter - editable
- Adult store clerk - assembled 2D art: individual editable graphic pieces.
- Counter approach - stealth arrival: move this marker to change the reassurance trigger location.
- Monster emergence - clear of counter: keep this point outside counter/wall collisions.
- Hidden Rampager - clerk tutorial: uses standard perception / movement / attack components.

Sequence:
1. Skylight starts open; gun requirement and existing roof destination remain intact.
2. On gameplay entry, invisible Rampager establishes watch, clerk says:
   Stay there.
   If you want to live, slowly walk to me.
3. Click or Space advances. Player, enemies and camera freeze; audio remains running.
   Closing input is consumed until release, so advancing dialogue cannot also shoot/jump.
4. Hint: Press SHIFT to stealth walk.
5. Normal running uses the existing accumulated 0.5-second watch anger threshold.
   On Attack, the Rampager becomes visible beside the counter and pursues normally.
6. Quiet arrival near the counter starts: Be nice... Go to sleep!
   Hidden observer sleeps, heartbeat stops, tutorial completion is remembered.
7. Existing water gun pickup and skylight exit remain available.
8. Retry before completion resets this encounter. Completion survives ordinary retry/travel;
   a full new game resets it. No new background music track was added (store has no authored BGM).

Reusable dialogue architecture:
- NpcDialogueSequence.cs: ScriptableObject containing speaker/text lines.
- RecoilNpcDialogue.cs: world runner, input, gameplay pause and bottom dialogue presentation.
- ServiceStoreEncounter.cs: scene story progression only.
- PlayerGazeFeedback.cs: heartbeat uses unscaled time during conversation.
- RecoilDoor.cs: startsOpened flag, independent of actual travel completion.

Dialogue content assets: Assets/RecoilPrototype/Dialogue/StoreClerk_*.asset
Inspector: dialogue lines are editable without changing the encounter script.
Hidden tutorial observer has extended authored sight distance and large watch stand-off so the
entrance/counter staging works in darkness; normal Rampager defaults were not changed.

Research decision:
Follow content / runner / presentation separation used by Yarn Spinner. For this small,
linear encounter a lightweight existing-project implementation avoids adding a package,
while preserving separate content assets and callbacks for future scripted NPCs.
https://docs.yarnspinner.dev/components/dialogue-runner
https://docs.yarnspinner.dev/2.2/using-yarnspinner-with-unity/components/dialogue-view/dialogue-advance-input
Do not pause AudioListener or set timeScale to zero; existing gameplay gates freeze simulation
and camera while the heartbeat continues independently. Unity documents global listener pause:
https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.6/script-reference/unityengine/audiolistener/pause
